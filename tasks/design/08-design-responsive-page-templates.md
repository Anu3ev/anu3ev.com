# DES-08: Design Responsive Page Templates

**Status:** Planned  
**Depends on:** DES-07

## Objective

Extend the validated system to all required page templates and responsive compositions.

## Deliverables

- Home, Work index, Case Study, Writing index, Article, About, Contact, Privacy, and 404 templates.
- Mobile menu and contact-form state screens.
- Mobile 390 px and desktop 1440 px compositions for every template.
- Tablet 768 px compositions where content flow changes materially.
- Dark/light and English/Russian validation variants.
- Responsive annotations for grid, order, wrapping, media, and navigation behaviour.

## Acceptance criteria

- Layouts remain usable from 320 px without horizontal overflow.
- Language switching preserves the equivalent page and does not fall back to Home.
- Long project and article titles do not break cards or navigation.
- Article and case-study reading widths remain comfortable.
- Missing optional media has a designed fallback.
- Search, complex filters, and a separate Services page are not introduced in the first version.
