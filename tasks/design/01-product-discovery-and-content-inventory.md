# DES-01: Complete Product Discovery and Content Inventory

**Status:** In Progress
**Depends on:** None

**Working documents:** [Project brief](../../docs/product/project-brief.md), [Content inventory](../../docs/product/content-inventory.md)

## Objective

Establish the factual product and content baseline before any visual work begins.

## Deliverables

- Current-domain inventory covering live URLs, indexed pages, redirects, analytics, Search Console, and known backlinks.
- Primary audience and hiring goal statement.
- Evidence inventory: experience, responsibilities, outcomes, metrics, testimonials, and public links.
- Inventory of at least three potential project case studies, including NDA limitations.
- Portrait, résumé, social links, and contact-channel inventory.
- Initial English and Russian source material without invented claims.

## Acceptance criteria

- The primary goal remains demonstrating expertise for international job search.
- Every proposed achievement has a verifiable source or is explicitly marked as unavailable.
- At least one project has enough information to design a complete representative case study.
- Missing content has an owner and a concrete collection action.
- Existing URLs that may require preservation are recorded.
