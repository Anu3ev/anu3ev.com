I worked with Alexander at InSales, where I was CTO and co-founder. Although I was not his direct manager, we interacted regularly and I had the opportunity to see his professional growth over several years.

Alexander joined the company in Technical Support, working directly with merchants and solving storefront and template issues. He quickly stood out for his technical ability and deep understanding of users’ problems, and later successfully moved into the frontend engineering team. He grew into a strong and independent engineer capable of owning complex product features from requirements and API design through implementation and release.

Among his most notable projects was a sophisticated browser-based image editor built with TypeScript and Fabric.js, now used by thousands of merchants. He also developed the frontend for a paid abandoned-cart product and contributed to the production migration from Vue 2 to Vue 3.

Alexander combines strong Vue and TypeScript expertise with valuable product thinking and a genuine understanding of users. I would gladly recommend him for a Senior Frontend Developer role, especially in a product team working on complex interfaces and real customer problems.
