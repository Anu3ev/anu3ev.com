I’d like to highlight two particularly strong projects:

This was a major initiative that took more than a year to develop and included shapes and backgrounds, text blocks, AI-powered features such as background removal and image enhancement, object manipulation, cropping, batch processing, template saving, and ready-to-use templates. Today, the tool is actively used by around 3,000 users.

— **Abandoned Carts:** a dedicated section with analytics, bulk and trigger-based campaigns, and detailed statistics. This is a paid feature for larger customers, currently used by around 200 online stores. It has already generated approximately twice the revenue needed to cover its development costs.
