# Project Brief

**Status:** Initial discovery

**Last updated:** 2026-08-24

## Product purpose

anu3ev.com is Alexander Anufriev's bilingual professional website. Its primary job is to help him find a strong international frontend role by showing the depth of his work, engineering judgment, product thinking, and ability to own complex browser-based features.

Qualified client enquiries, professional authority, and technical writing are secondary goals. They must not weaken the hiring signal or make the site look like a generic agency landing page.

## Audience priority

1. Recruiters evaluating Alexander for international remote frontend roles.
2. Engineering and product leaders evaluating technical depth, ownership, and decision quality.
3. Founders or agencies considering a project or consulting engagement.
4. Developers discovering the site through technical writing and open-source work.

## Proof hierarchy

The website should prove the positioning in this order:

1. Detailed project case studies with context, constraints, decisions, and outcomes.
2. Public work such as the Fabric Image Editor repository, package, and demo.
3. Specific experience and verified scale from inSales.
4. A first-hand recommendation from a colleague.
5. Technical articles grounded in real engineering work.
6. A concise skills list supporting the evidence above.

The site must not lead with a long technology cloud or unsupported business claims.

## Localization

- English is the source language for new website content.
- Codex performs the first English review; Alexander performs the final review.
- Russian content is written during implementation rather than copied from an existing content archive.
- Every published page, case study, and article must have complete English and Russian versions.
- The design must tolerate longer Russian strings and at least 30 percent text expansion.

## Visual direction

- Start from an original baseline rather than copying reference websites.
- Review the first baseline critically and iterate from concrete feedback.
- Use an editorial engineering visual language, mobile-first composition, restrained motion, and reusable tile-based layouts.
- Dark mode is the default. A complete light mode is required.
- Use a muted copper-orange accent on warm neutral surfaces rather than bright red, neon orange, glow-heavy effects, or generic AI portfolio motifs.
- Use the portrait as supporting evidence of a real person, not as the main visual proof.

- The visual and written result must feel authored rather than generated.
