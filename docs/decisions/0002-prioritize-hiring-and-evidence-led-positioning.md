# 0002: Prioritize Hiring and Evidence-led Positioning

**Status:** Accepted

**Date:** 2026-08-24

## Context

The website may support job search, consulting enquiries, professional authority, and technical writing. Giving all goals equal weight would produce an unfocused portfolio and unclear calls to action.

## Decision

- Make international job search the primary product goal.
- Position Alexander as a product-oriented frontend engineer specializing in Vue, TypeScript, complex product UI, e-commerce SaaS, and browser-based editors.
- Use detailed project evidence before skills lists or broad claims.
- Use the Fabric Image Editor and inSales Infographics Editor as the first representative case.
- Keep client enquiries and the blog as secondary paths.
- Offer email, LinkedIn, and Telegram as public contact methods.

## Consequences

- The homepage prioritizes selected work and the hiring signal before services or blog promotion.
- The primary action leads to project evidence; contact remains a visible secondary action.
- Case studies must explain decisions and outcomes rather than act as screenshot galleries.
- Claims must follow the verification rules in the content guidelines.
