## Responsibility boundaries

- Product documents describe the current known truth and may be updated as new evidence appears.
- Decision records explain why a durable choice was made. Accepted records are not silently rewritten when the choice changes; a later record supersedes them.
- Tasks describe work that still needs to be performed. They must link to product documents or decisions instead of duplicating those documents.
- Production content will live in the Nuxt content structure after the content system is implemented.

## Source precedence

When sources disagree, use this order:

1. Alexander's latest explicit correction.
2. The canonical candidate profile from the tailor-alexander-resume skill.
3. The verified achievement and inSales experience banks from that skill.
4. The latest general resume and selected-work materials.
5. Older notes, interview drafts, and vacancy-specific applications.

Never resolve a meaningful conflict by choosing the stronger marketing claim. Record the conflict and request confirmation.
