Privacy

What happens to contact form data

This prototype does not send or store form submissions. The production form will be enabled only after its processor and retention period are documented here.

Information the form collects

The form asks for your name, email address, reason for contacting me, an optional company name, and the message you choose to send.

How it will be used

The information will be used to understand and reply to your message, prevent form abuse, and investigate delivery failures. It will not be sold, added to an unrelated mailing list, or used to build an advertising profile.

Before the production form goes live

This page will name the service that processes submissions, its hosting region where applicable, the retention period for messages and technical logs, and the method for requesting access or deletion.

Contact without the form

You can avoid the form and email alexander.s.anufriev@gmail.com directly.
