Theme validation

Dark and light are not mechanical inversions

Light uses softer borders and a darker brand action. Dark uses a lighter copper action and carefully stepped graphite surfaces.

DARK

Comfortable graphite

Primary #F7F3EE on canvas #110E0C. Secondary #BDAFA2 remains readable without competing with headings.

LIGHT

Warm paper, not white glare

Primary #191512 on canvas #F7F3EE. Secondary #574A41 keeps body text strong.

Typography

Manrope for product clarity, IBM Plex Mono for evidence

Both families support Latin and Cyrillic. Russian content keeps the same sizes and is allowed to wrap naturally.
